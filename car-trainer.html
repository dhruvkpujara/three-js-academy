Record a complete lap with varied steering and throttle, then train the neural network.

This page trains a small neural network (6 inputs → 8 hidden neurons → 2 outputs) in-browser using backpropagation. The geometry-aware autopilot follows each distinct circuit; one lap teaches the network your driving style, but cannot guarantee perfect learning of every unseen track.

Sensor distances are simulated track-edge ray measurements in pixels. Speed and distance are simulation units, not real-world vehicle measurements. Use this as a learning sandbox, not a real vehicle controller.
