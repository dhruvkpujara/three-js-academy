Drag parts to move them. In Wire mode, click one terminal and then another. Terminals are intentionally large for touch screens.

How scoring works

Correct circuits award points once per level: 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 points. This starter build checks component counts and whether the circuit forms a connected network; it does not yet simulate real voltage or current.

Tip: A real parallel circuit has separate branches connected across the same two nodes. Don't connect every lamp end-to-end.
